About

Analytical thinking shaped by data, physics, and research

I am curious about what lies beneath the surface—how systems work, why patterns emerge, and how apparently separate events may be connected. Physics and mathematics taught me to approach complex questions through evidence and logic; biophotonics research taught me how careful processing can turn abstract measurements into a meaningful story. Today, I apply that mindset to business data by investigating patterns and anomalies, validating results, and building reports and dashboards that people can understand and use. I am completing an M.S. in Technology Management with a concentration in Engineering Operations and am open to data analyst, BI analyst, reporting analyst, and related roles.
